Features

Everything your farm gate needs

Proof that every visitor was screened and disinfected, without anyone standing guard.

Contactless screening

An infrared thermometer checks each person's temperature. Anyone with a fever is stopped at the gate automatically.

Automatic disinfection

The booth senses each person and runs the misting cycle by itself, and records whether it finished.

Live monitoring

See every entry seconds after it happens, today's totals, and whether the booth is online.

Instant alerts

Get notified on your phone about high temperatures, denied entries, incomplete disinfection or a booth that goes offline.

Reports in one click

Daily, weekly and monthly biosecurity reports with charts, ready to print or download as Excel.

Team access

Give administrators and farm managers their own accounts, with every action recorded in an audit log.

How it works

From the gate to your phone in seconds

1

Step in

A person enters the disinfection booth at the farm gate.

2

Screen

Their temperature is checked and entry is granted or denied.

3

Disinfect

The booth mists them with disinfectant automatically.

4

Stay informed

The entry appears in the app, and you are alerted if something is wrong.

Mobile app

Put DisinfEntry on your phone

No app store needed. It installs straight from your browser in a few taps.

Android

  1. Open DisinfEntry in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right), then Install app.
  3. Open DisinfEntry from your home screen.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open DisinfEntry in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
